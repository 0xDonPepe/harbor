# Harbor 0.35.0

Harbor still does one thing at a time, but you no longer wait to ask for the next one. Nothing is greyed out because
something else is running: what you ask for joins a **queue** you can see and manage.

- **No more greyed-out buttons.** Before, any running operation (an install, an update, a nightly backup) disabled
  Install, Update, Remove and the rest on every app. Now a button is disabled only for its own reason (a form that
  is not filled in, a name to type).
- **Queue actions on a busy app.** While an app installs, updates or waits its turn, its page still offers *Start*,
  *Stop*, *Restart*, *Update*, *Remove*, *Reinstall* and *Uninstall completely* (whichever make sense after what is
  queued). Harbor asks once (*Queue: Update Immich*) and makes the plan when the item's turn comes, against the app
  as it is then. If the plan needs something only you can give (a new permission, a value to type, a passphrase),
  the item stops as **Needs your review** and nothing changes. Other apps' actions are reviewed and approved as before
  and simply wait their turn.
- **The queue.** The tray at the bottom right now lists the running item with its progress, the waiting items in the
  order they will run, and finished items with their results (passwords and recovery words still shown once). Fold
  it to one line, close an item with ×, or **Clear finished**.
- **Cancel.** A waiting item can be cancelled before it starts, and nothing is changed: a cancelled install frees its
  name and ports, and a cancelled adopt leaves the app on its drive ready to adopt again. A running step cannot be
  stopped halfway. Items Harbor queued to protect an app (a stop because its drive disappeared, a link repair, a
  backup's last pass) cannot be cancelled; the queue says why.
- **Restarts.** If Harbor restarts or stops, items that had not started are cancelled (nothing was changed, queue them
  again) instead of marking their apps *Needs attention*. The one that was running is still *Needs attention*, as
  before.
- **Update all** now queues apps that are busy instead of skipping them.
- **CLI.** `harbor queue` lists the queue (`--json`), and `harbor queue cancel <operation>` cancels a waiting item.
  On a busy app, `harbor start|stop|restart|update|remove|reinstall|purge` offers to queue the action (`--yes`
  accepts). With `--accept-permissions`, `--passphrase-stdin` or values, it waits for the app as before.
  `harbor start` on a locked app now asks for the passphrase (or reads `--passphrase-stdin`) and unlocks it.
- **API.** `GET /v1/operations` (the queue), `POST /v1/operations` with `{action: {kind, instanceId}}` (queue an
  action without a plan), `POST /v1/operations/:id/cancel`. Operations gain the `cancelled` state and `actor`,
  `instanceName`, `queuedAction` and `cancellable` fields; `planId` may be null. New error codes: `NOT_CANCELLABLE`,
  `REVIEW_NEEDED`, `CANCELLED`.
- New accounts cannot use one of Harbor's own actor names (`scheduler`, `auto-update`, `drive-guard`, …) as their
  username.

Database schema 13 (the `operations` table is rebuilt once at the first start; nothing to do). No change to the
package format.

Already running Harbor? Settings → Overview → Update, or `sudo /opt/harbor/bin/harbor self-update apply --to 0.35.0`.
